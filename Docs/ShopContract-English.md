The agreement may be renewed every six months as needed and shall continue for as long as the parties consider appropriate.

## 3. Tenant's obligations

1. Use the shop only for business that is lawful under the law.
2. Do not sublet the rented shop to another person without the Landlord's consent.
3. Do nothing that causes a nuisance to the property owner or neighbours.
4. Keep the premises clean and in satisfactory condition at all times and carry out minor repairs.
5. Pay all utility and service charges incurred through use of the premises.
6. Make no internal or external alterations without consulting the Landlord.
7. Notify the Landlord of an intention not to continue the agreement thirty (30) days before its expiry.

## 4. Landlord's undertaking

For as long as the Tenant fulfils the obligations under this agreement, the Landlord shall allow the Tenant to conduct business peacefully, without disturbance from the Landlord or anyone claiming through the Landlord.

## System interpretation

The six-month instalment implies a monthly reporting equivalent of **TZS 250,000**, calculated as 1,500,000 ÷ 6. This calculation is an implementation interpretation, not an additional charge in the original text. The original has no security deposit clause; a deposit shown in a generated agreement is an additional configured term and should be zero unless agreed by the parties. The system requires dated leases; the source leaves the exact date and ongoing duration open, so the parties must supply the agreed start and end dates.
